## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one production-ready raster illustration for a Korean university timetable website's friendly self-invitation notice, displayed above HTML text on desktop and mobile.
Primary request: a cute anthropomorphic weekly timetable card cheerfully trying to high-five its own reflection in a freestanding oval mirror, then realizing it is itself. One real character and the matching mirrored reflection only, no second independent character. The card has softly rounded corners, a rose-red top strip, a simple three-by-three timetable grid of pastel blocks on its belly, tiny dot eyes, rosy cheeks and short rounded arms and legs. The raised hand touches the mirror at exactly the reflected hand; the mirrored grid and red strip clearly match. Small bashful surprise motion marks communicate a gentle 'oops, that's me' feeling.
Style/medium: polished flat editorial mascot illustration, warm hand-drawn charcoal outlines, simple readable silhouettes, restrained soft cel shading, rounded playful shapes; friendly rather than an alarming error.
Composition/framing: centered compact scene, square 1024 x 1024 canvas, full character and full mirror visible, generous clear padding, reads clearly at 280px width.
Color palette: OTL rose-red #E54C65 accents, soft cream card, muted blush pink, pale blue mirror glass, charcoal outlines; harmonious on both light and dark web interfaces.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with alpha, no rectangular backdrop, no floor or room scene; only a small subtle contact shadow under the objects.
Constraints: no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no UI screenshot, no text whatsoever; website will render Korean copy separately. Original character, not an existing brand mascot.
